# Curry and rice

## Ingredients
- rice, 600 g
- onion, 2
- curry roux, 100 g
- water, 800 ml

## Steps
1. Rinse rice, then soak for 30 min, then cook.
2. Peel onion, then cut into slices (3 mm), then fry over medium heat for 10 min.
3. Simmer onion, water, curry roux over low heat for 20 min.
4. Plate cooked rice, curry.
